# When You See "You Are in Violation of the Terms of Use"

This page provides guidance on how to handle the following error message that appears while browsing the site.

> **Example Messages Displayed**
> You are in violation of the terms of use
> An ad blocker was detected in your browser.
> By using the Search3958 site, you are deemed to have agreed to the terms of use.
> To avoid many issues, please allow ads or withdraw your agreement to the terms of use.

---

## Solutions

### 1. If You Are Using an Ad Blocker (Extension)

To use the site, you need to disable the ad blocker or add this site to the exclusion list.

* **How to Change Settings**
* In your browser's extension settings, register this site (Search3958) in the "Allowlist" or "Exceptions" list.
* If you want to remove the extension, uninstall it from the Chrome Web Store or similar.
* You can also access the Chrome Web Store from the "Allow Ads" button on the error screen.



---

### 2. If You Are Not Using an Ad Blocker / The Issue Persists Even When Disabled

Your browser's privacy and security features or tracking prevention features may be affecting the site.

* **Check Browser Security & Privacy Settings**
* **If you are using Microsoft Edge**
> Changing the tracking prevention intensity from "Strict" to "Basic" or "Balanced" in the "Privacy, search, and services" settings menu may resolve the issue.
* **If you are using other browsers (Chrome / Safari / Firefox, etc.)**
> Similarly, excessive tracking prevention features or restrictions on third-party cookies may be the cause. Temporarily relax the security level and try again.



* **If a screen saying "You have withdrawn consent" is displayed**
> Click "Agree to Terms of Use" on the screen to return to the original page.
